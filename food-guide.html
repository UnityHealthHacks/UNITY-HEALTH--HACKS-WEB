Food guide

Build meals from affordable, flexible foods.

There is no required brand, powder, or “detox kit.” This page is an educational starting point, not an individualized meal prescription.

Protein anchors

Eggs, beans, lentils, yogurt, cottage cheese, tofu, fish, poultry, lean meats, or other suitable choices.

Fiber-rich plants

Oats, barley, beans, vegetables, fruit, whole grains, nuts, and seeds—introduced gradually.

Flavor and fats

Olive oil, avocado, nuts, seeds, herbs, spices, citrus, and simple sauces.

Simple meal formula

PartExamples
1 proteinEggs, beans, fish, chicken, tofu, yogurt
2 plantsLeafy greens, carrots, tomatoes, berries, broccoli, peppers
1 fiber-rich carbohydrateOats, potato, brown rice, quinoa, beans, whole-grain bread
1 practical fatOlive oil, nuts, seeds, avocado

Budget starter list

Oats, eggs, dried or canned beans, lentils, frozen vegetables, bananas, apples, plain yogurt, potatoes, canned tuna or salmon, brown rice, peanut butter, onions, garlic, and seasonal produce.

Individual needs differ: Allergies, intolerances, kidney disease, diabetes treatment, digestive disorders, and other medical conditions may require changes. Use qualified care when your medical situation affects food choices.
