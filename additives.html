9.3 DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE — NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE YET. Reconciled from the protected 9.2 baseline; review before deployment.

Learning topic

Ingredient-by-ingredient, country-by-country

Draft educational architecture for evidence review and plain-language publication.

The project will compare additives individually rather than calling every processed ingredient “toxic.” Regulations, approved uses, exposure, and evidence differ.

Colors and dyes

Review specific color additives, behavioral evidence, exposure, labeling, and differences among jurisdictions.

Preservatives

Explain what they do, why they are used, typical exposure, and where evidence supports concern or safety limits.

Texture and stability

Emulsifiers, gums, carrageenan, and other ingredients require specific evidence—not blanket conclusions.

Sweeteners

Added sugars and artificial or non-nutritive sweeteners should be compared by type, dose, goals, and evidence.

Other ingredients

Titanium dioxide, brominated ingredients, and country-specific restrictions require current regulatory research before publication.

Practical rule

Users should be able to identify simpler alternatives without fear-based language or perfectionism.

Publication note: each major factual claim will require current, traceable sources and a review date before launch.
