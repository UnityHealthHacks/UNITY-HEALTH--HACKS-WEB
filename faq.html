Frequently asked questions

Clear answers and honest limits.

Is Unity Health Hacks medical care?

No. It provides general education and personal-experience-based guidance with evidence and safety limits.

Is the $19.99 checkout active?

No. The price is a current direction. Payment is not active until Stripe and the complete access flow are connected and tested.

Does the Food Checker diagnose reactions?

No. It explains entered label information and ingredient functions. It cannot determine whether a food caused a symptom.

Are the 60- and 90-day programs final?

No. Their architecture is preserved, but detailed day-by-day curriculum requires final evidence and safety review.

Where is my information stored?

Current preview tools use only local browser storage. A production portal will require secure authentication, clear privacy disclosures, and controlled data storage.
