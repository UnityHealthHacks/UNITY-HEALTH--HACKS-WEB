Education without unsupported cleanse claims

Draft educational architecture for evidence review and plain-language publication.

Parasites

Risk depends on organism, geography, travel, water, food, sanitation, animal exposure, and other factors. Symptoms overlap with many non-parasitic conditions. Testing and treatment are organism-specific.

Useful education

  • Exposure prevention, safe food and water, sanitation
  • Testing limitations and repeat testing in selected cases
  • Organism-specific prescription treatment when indicated
  • Prevention of reinfection
  • Public-health deworming programs in endemic settings

Do not claim

  • Everyone has parasites
  • A seed, herb, binder, or cleanse eliminates an infection
  • Symptoms alone prove infection
  • Routine deworming is appropriate for every U.S. adult

Candida

Candida can cause real medical conditions, but broad online symptom lists are not diagnostic. “Candida cleanse” claims often exceed evidence.

Safety: Persistent oral, skin, genital, digestive, or systemic concerns deserve appropriate evaluation. Immunocompromised people require particular caution.

Publication note: each major factual claim will require current, traceable sources and a review date before launch.
