Ask Guardian Lab

Do not enter private medical records, passwords, medication lists, lab reports, or identifying health information. This is a local prototype, not a secure member record.

What this prototype enforces

  • Safety overrides before ordinary route or sales language
  • Medication-change requests never receive stop/reduce/dose instructions
  • Facts before assumptions
  • Known, uncertain, and missing information are separated
  • No diagnosis from symptoms, labels, photos, journals, or chat
  • No claim that an external database, clinician, live AI model, or product record is connected unless it actually is
  • Higher-safety questions do not automatically become higher-priced routes
