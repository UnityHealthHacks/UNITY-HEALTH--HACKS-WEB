Guardian Lab is a playable development prototype for the future Unity Health Hacks companion. It demonstrates tone, evidence labels, safety boundaries, follow-up questions, and transparent limits. It does not diagnose, prescribe, or replace medical care.

Ask Guardian Lab

Do not enter private medical records, passwords, or identifying health information. This prototype uses no cloud AI and stores nothing unless your browser itself retains page state.

What this prototype enforces

  • Facts before assumptions
  • Personal experience is not universal proof
  • Known, uncertain, and missing information are separated
  • No diagnosis from symptoms, labels, photos, journals, or chat
  • No instructions to stop or change prescribed medication
  • No claim that an external database, clinician, lab, or product record is connected unless it actually is
  • Red flags are routed to appropriate evaluation
  • Controversial questions can be investigated without being promoted as established facts
