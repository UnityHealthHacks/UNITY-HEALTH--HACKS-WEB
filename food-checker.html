Food and ingredient tools

Understand a food or ingredient in plain language.

Enter label details and ingredients. The result explains what stands out and suggests useful comparisons based on the information entered. It does not label a food universally good or bad, diagnose symptoms, or determine what is appropriate for every person.

TEST FEATURE

Barcode scanner / product code

Scan a UPC/EAN barcode with your phone camera when the browser supports native barcode detection, or type the number manually. The scanner captures the code only. A live product database is not connected yet, so a barcode is never treated as verified product information by itself.

Camera scanning generally requires HTTPS or localhost. Manual barcode entry works without a product database.
Optional considerations

What it should explain

Serving size, added sugar, sodium, fiber, protein, ingredient purpose, and product-code context when entered.

What it must not do

Diagnose parasites, Candida, diabetes, reflux, intolerance, or claim a food caused a condition.

Future AI direction

Future connected versions may identify a verified product from a barcode, interpret package labels, compare alternatives, and answer goal-based questions using transparent evidence and limits.
