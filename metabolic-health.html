Learning topic

Improvement, prevention, and remission—used precisely

Draft educational architecture for evidence review and plain-language publication.

Insulin resistance, prediabetes, and type 2 diabetes require careful language. Lifestyle changes may improve markers and, for some people, support remission, but “cure” or guaranteed reversal language is inappropriate.

Education priorities

  • Whole-food patterns and reduced refined carbohydrate load
  • Protein, fiber, movement, sleep, weight management when relevant
  • Objective measurements and clinician follow-up
  • Affordable, sustainable changes
  • Distinguish prevention, improvement, remission, and recurrence

Non-negotiable safety

  • Never stop diabetes medication without clinician supervision
  • Fasting can be unsafe with some medications or conditions
  • Symptoms alone do not measure glucose control
  • Urgent symptoms require prompt care

Publication note: each major factual claim will require current, traceable sources and a review date before launch.
