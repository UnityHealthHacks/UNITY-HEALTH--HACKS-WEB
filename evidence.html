9.3 DEVELOPMENT CANDIDATE — NOT FOR PUBLIC RELEASE YET. Reconciled from the protected 9.2 baseline; review before deployment.

Integrity is non-negotiable

Evidence first. Assumptions never.

Every important claim should be traceable, qualified, dated, and open to correction.

Established evidence

Supported by strong, relevant evidence and represented without exaggeration.

Emerging evidence

Promising but incomplete findings, clearly labeled as preliminary or uncertain.

Conflicting or limited evidence

Explain disagreement, study limitations, populations, and what remains unknown.

Personal experience

May explain the founder’s story, but it is not proof of cause, universal benefit, or guaranteed outcome.

Safety concerns

Interactions, contraindications, red flags, and when professional evaluation matters.

Corrections

Update or remove content when evidence, regulation, or verified information changes.

Claims the project will not publish

  • Guaranteed cures, detoxes, parasite elimination, or universal gut healing
  • Advice to stop medication or replace qualified medical care
  • Unsupported claims that an ingredient harms everyone
  • Claims that a country, agency, or industry hides cures without documentation
  • Fake credentials or certification language that implies a medical license
  • Features, programs, or payments described as working before testing
