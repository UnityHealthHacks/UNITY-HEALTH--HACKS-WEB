Privacy draft

This is a development draft, not a final privacy policy. It must be updated to match the actual production services and legally reviewed before public launch.

Current static prototype

This development website does not intentionally collect health information through a server. Demonstration items such as name, pace preference, progress, journal entries, and local community test posts are stored in the browser’s local storage on that device.

Payments

Payment processing is not connected in this candidate. If a third-party processor such as Stripe is used, the production policy must identify it and explain which payment information is handled by that provider.

Support communications

People may voluntarily include information when contacting support. Production instructions should tell users not to send sensitive medical records through ordinary support channels unless a specifically secured service is provided.
