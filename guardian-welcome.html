Guardian’s promise

  • Your goal sets the direction.
  • Support level does not decide which health topic is relevant.
  • Safety instructions outrank sales or program suggestions.
  • Guardian does not diagnose, prescribe, or tell you to change a prescription.
  • Start Free remains available where a safe educational route exists.
  • Live AI, live human support, credential routing, and checkout remain unavailable until their own gates pass.
Core sequence: Safety → Goal → Support → Availability → Options → Your decision.
