Meet Guardian

I’m here to help you explore the direction you want to go.

Tell Guardian what you want to work on. Guardian gives fact-based options, explains what each path includes, and leaves the decision with you.

Start with you

This prototype stores your name, goal, and selected path only in this browser using local storage. Do not enter private medical records here.

No-pressure rule: If an option feels too expensive or too involved, choose a lower support level and Guardian will narrow the visible paths again. Higher tiers stay hidden until your own request makes them relevant.

Guardian’s promise

  • Your goal sets the direction.
  • Facts and sources guide the options.
  • Guardian does not diagnose or prescribe.
  • Higher-support tiers add research, tools, or qualified human help—not stronger promises.
  • You can change or upgrade paths later.
Core sequence: Facts → Sources → Context → Options → Your decision.

Your suggested paths

How matching works in this prototype: Guardian uses the support level you select plus plain-language keywords in your stated goal to surface only the most relevant starter path(s). It does not diagnose, and it does not use affiliate commission to rank products or support paths.
