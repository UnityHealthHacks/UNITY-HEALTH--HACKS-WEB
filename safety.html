Safety

Read this before making significant changes.

Unity Health Hacks is educational. Urgent symptoms, medication decisions, and medical conditions belong with appropriate qualified care.

Emergency: Seek emergency medical help for severe or rapidly worsening symptoms.

Speak with a qualified professional before significant changes when you:

  • are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • have kidney, liver, heart, gastrointestinal, immune, or endocrine disease
  • take prescription medicine, especially diabetes drugs, blood thinners, blood-pressure drugs, seizure medicine, psychiatric medicine, or transplant medicine
  • have a history of an eating disorder
  • are preparing for surgery
  • are making decisions for a child or dependent adult

Do not use this platform to justify:

  • stopping or reducing prescribed medicine without the prescriber
  • using veterinary antiparasitic drugs
  • combining multiple herbs or supplements without appropriate interaction review
  • using activated charcoal near medicines unless medically directed
  • assuming worsening symptoms are a “die-off” reaction

This safety page was recovered from an earlier source and retained because it reinforces current UHH governance: education first, no diagnosis, no medication discontinuation advice, and no unsafe detox or parasite-treatment assumptions.
