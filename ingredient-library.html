Open Food Guide
Added sugars
Identify common names, compare grams per serving, and consider frequency and overall dietary pattern.
Natural flavors
A broad flavoring category. The label does not list every component, but the term alone does not prove harm.
Maltodextrin
A starch-derived ingredient used for texture, bulking, or processing. Context and amount matter.
Gums and thickeners
Ingredients such as guar gum or xanthan gum help texture and stability; individual tolerance may differ.
Emulsifiers
Help oil and water remain mixed. Evidence should be evaluated ingredient by ingredient.
Nitrites and nitrates
Used in some cured meats. Explain use, exposure, food context, and available alternatives.
Hydrogenated oils
Differentiate fully hydrogenated from partially hydrogenated oils and review the complete label.
