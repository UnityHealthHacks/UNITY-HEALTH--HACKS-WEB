Future learning and coach development

Unity Health Hacks University

A structured educational pathway built around evidence literacy, communication, practical support, ethics, safety, privacy, and clear limits.

Learning pathways

  • Food labels and ingredient education
  • Digestive, metabolic, nutrition, movement, sleep, and habit fundamentals
  • Established evidence, emerging ideas, conflicting findings, and unsupported claims
  • Plain-language communication and behavior support
  • Practical implementation for individuals and families

Coach pathway standards

  • Required courses and assessments
  • Ethics, privacy, safety, and referral boundaries
  • Practical requirements and supervision where required
  • Complaints, renewal, verification, and revocation systems
  • No diagnosis, prescribing, medication management, treatment, or implied medical license

Private program credential

After completing the required curriculum, assessments, ethics and safety training, and any required practical work, a candidate may qualify for a Unity Health Hacks Certified Lifestyle Coach certificate, digital credential, verification number, and physical plaque.

Required disclosure: This would be a private Unity Health Hacks educational credential. It would not be a government license and would not authorize medical diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, or medical care.

Professional network architecture

Qualified roles

Lifestyle coaches, family coaches, meal-planning/meal-prep support, approved professionals, and licensed clinicians remain separate roles with visible scope and credential rules.

Lead routing

Future routing uses ZIP/city → county → state → service territory. Member contact information is shared only after explicit consent and only to the minimum extent needed. Test lifecycle: NEW → MATCHED → ACCEPTED → CONTACTED → SCHEDULED → COMPLETED, with audit history.

Affiliate participation

Approved participants may receive unique UHH referral identifiers mapped to approved vendor program IDs. Sale attribution, holds, refunds, reversals, participant share, UHH share, and payout records must remain traceable. Commission tracking stays separate from Guardian's evidence and product-ranking logic.

Healthcare referral rule: Do not activate compensation for patient referrals or other regulated professional relationships until the specific arrangement has appropriate legal review.
