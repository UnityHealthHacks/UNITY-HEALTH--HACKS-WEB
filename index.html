Truthful development boundaries

What 9.5 does—and does not—claim.

Start Free stays visible

Goal routing does not require payment, and support level does not determine which health goal is treated as relevant.

The current Guardian Lab is a local rule-based prototype. It does not claim a live AI model, secure member account, clinician, or product database is connected.

No live checkout, paid higher-safety program, credential/provider routing, or unfinished service is activated in this development build.

Our promise: No cure claims, no guaranteed outcomes, no fear selling, no surprise upsells, and no unfinished feature presented as complete.
