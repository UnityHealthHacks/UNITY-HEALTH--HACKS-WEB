Start Here

A calm, practical beginning.

This prototype organizes the experience a new member would see before beginning any program.

1. Know what this is

Unity Health Hacks is educational. It helps explain foods, labels, habits, and questions to discuss with a qualified clinician. It is not diagnosis, treatment, or a guaranteed outcome.

2. Choose your pace

Begin with the 30-day foundation. Continue only with habits that are safe, tolerable, affordable, and sustainable for you.

3. Track what matters

Record food, energy, sleep, digestive comfort, movement, and questions. A journal helps you notice patterns without assuming cause.

4. Use medical care appropriately

Persistent, severe, new, or worsening symptoms require appropriate evaluation. Medication changes require clinician supervision.

Before beginning

Review

Current conditions, medications, pregnancy, eating-disorder history, kidney or heart concerns, diabetes treatment, and other factors can change what is safe.

Prepare

Choose ordinary whole foods, a water bottle, a simple journal, and realistic movement. Expensive products are not required.

Keep perspective

The goal is not perfection. It is learning, consistency, and better-informed choices.
