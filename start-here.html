Start Here

A calm, practical beginning—based on your goal.

You do not have to begin with the same program as everyone else. Start with what you want to accomplish, then choose the amount of structure that fits your life.

Choose My GoalUse Guardian Goal Routing

1. Know what this is

Unity Health Hacks is educational. It helps explain foods, labels, habits, and questions to discuss with an appropriate qualified clinician. It is not diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, or a guaranteed outcome.

2. Choose a goal first

Weight, general lifestyle, gut/microbiome, digestive comfort, blood pressure, lipids, metabolic health, fasting, pet/parasite exposure, food/ingredients, sleep/movement/hydration, or Help Me Choose can each start from a different educational route.

3. Choose your support

Learn for free, use a self-guided path, or select more structure when an appropriate program actually exists. Unbuilt live AI, paid higher-safety routes, live human support, and checkout remain unavailable.

4. Use medical care appropriately

Safety can override ordinary routing. Medication changes require appropriate clinician involvement, and urgent or concerning features should not be pushed through a lifestyle-sales funnel.

Where the 30-Day Foundation fits

Strong general-lifestyle option

The 30-Day Foundation can provide broad habit structure when your primary goal is general lifestyle improvement.

Not a universal prescription

It is not automatically the correct first path for reflux, blood-pressure questions, medication decisions, parasite exposure, or every other goal.

Days 31–90 remain development work

Detailed Days 31–90 are not presented as complete until they are actually researched, reviewed, built, and verified.

Before using a higher-safety practice

UHH asks path-specific safety questions only when they are relevant. It does not require every visitor to submit a diagnosis list, medication list, labs, or detailed medical history just to receive an educational route.

Privacy boundary: Do not use the current local prototype as a secure medical record. Sensitive production health storage requires separate authentication, privacy, security, retention, consent, and access controls.
